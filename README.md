# Findable Check

A one-page checklist for students of **Retail & Digital Marketing — Argentina 2026, Lesson 4 (Be found: from search to AI answers)**.

Students paste a customer's question, the brand name, the page title, the summary at the top, the article and the FAQ. The tool checks the writing against the Lesson 4 rules, part by part:

**1 · The top of the page:** the customer's words · title length · answer first · a short summary · the title as a question · a first sentence that names the brand.
**2 · The article:** clear headings · headings in the customer's words · each heading answered at once · short paragraphs · enough to answer · facts in lists · facts in the text · few empty marketing words · short sentences · who wrote it and when.
**3 · The FAQ:** real questions with short answers.

Each check is tagged *For Google*, *For the AI* or both, and gives two scores: rules passed for Google (out of 10) and for the AI (out of 11). The Google checks combine Google's own guidance (write for people, clear titles and headings, no keyword stuffing) with good-writing habits; Google publishes no word-count rule.

It is a writing checklist, not a ranking tool: it does not call Google or any AI, and nothing typed leaves the browser.

Languages: English and Spanish (button top right, or add `#es` to the URL).

## Publish on GitHub Pages
1. Create a repository (e.g. `findablecheck`) under the `parliamodimarketing` account.
2. Upload `index.html` and this README.
3. Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root.
4. The page will be at `https://parliamodimarketing.github.io/findablecheck/` (Spanish: add `#es`).
